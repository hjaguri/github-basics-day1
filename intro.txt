1
Keep It Simple: Avoid lengthy sentences; use abbreviations and keywords to condense information. 
2
Use Your Own Words: Writing in your own words helps you understand the material better. 
1
Highlight Key Points: Use color coding or underlining to emphasize important information. 
1
Organize by Topic: Structure your notes using bullet points or numbered lists for clarity. 
1

These strategies will help you create concise and effective notes for better retention and understanding.
